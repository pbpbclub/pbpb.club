<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user, a furniture production business owner, wants an internal application named Factory Hub to manage orders and calculate costs.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
- **Goal:** Track orders, manage a production calendar, and calculate planned vs. actual costs.
- **Data Models:**
    - **Order:** Contains details like ID, name, client, dates, status, and costs.
    - **Stages:** Orders go through sequential stages (Project, Estimate, Production), with production having sub-stages (Welding, Painting, etc.).
- **Cost Calculation:**
    - Costs are tracked per stage (materials + labor).
    - Sale price = Material cost + 60% markup.
    - A separate, higher price for non-cash payments ().
    - A material cost database is required.
- **Production Calendar:**
    - Must have Day/Week/Month views and a Gantt chart.
    - Needs to visualize production stages, order timelines, and potential bottlenecks.
    - Requires drag-and-drop for rescheduling.
- **Interface:**
    - Must be minimalist, utilitarian, and fast.
    - Main screens: Order List, Order Card, Calendar.
- **New Feature: Clients (Заказчики):**
    - A dedicated section to manage client information, including contact details, payment terms, and order history.
- **General Functionality:**
    - Add filters to all tables.
    - Add a delete function (trash can icon) for orders, clients, and materials.

**User's preferred language**: Русский (Russian)
